How to play

A private tracker for nail biting, skin picking and hair pulling: log an urge in two taps, see your patterns and grow a small world on good days.

Body-focused repetitive behaviors (BFRB) such as trichotillomania and dermatillomania are hard to notice while they happen. Grovly makes noticing quick: tap the behavior, then choose Resisted or Did it. Add a body area, intensity, trigger or note if you like, or skip them.

Two Ways To Count Progress

  • Reduce: every Did it is counted, and each day is compared with your own recent average. Fewer than usual is progress.
  • Strict streak: only a clean day counts, and one Did it resets the streak. Your longest streak stays saved.

Switch any time in Settings. Switching never deletes or rewrites your history.

Seeds And Plants

Each good day grows your world by one step. Resisting an urge earns a seed, and you choose what to plant. The world follows the calendar: spring, summer, autumn and winter change the colors and the plants. A hard stretch pauses growth, and nothing is ever taken away. No spins, no loot boxes, and nothing to buy for progress.

In The Moment

Tap I have an urge for four free tools: box or 4-7-8 breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, Hands (make fists and hold for a minute) and a 90-second wait. You can log the outcome afterwards or close the flow.

See What Changes

A month calendar of day rings shows every day at a glance: one ring segment per log, and a slip day marked in the center. Below it are the selected day's logs and a year strip. Daily, weekly and monthly charts compare this week with last week and show the hours when urges come most often. Your full history is kept, and you can correct or remove any entry.

Optional Caffeine Log

Turn on the caffeine log in Settings if you want it. Log a drink against a daily limit you choose; 400 mg is general guidance for adults. The log is free, local and separate from your progress.

Light, Evening And Auto

Pick Light, Evening or Auto in Settings. Auto follows the time of day and System follows your phone. All themes are free.

Private By Design

  • No account, no sign-up, no email.
  • Your data stays on your phone. No ads.
  • Back up to a file and restore it on a new phone.
  • One daily reminder at the time you choose.

What Is Free

Tracking is free and unlimited: all three behaviors, every body area from fingers and cuticles to scalp and eyebrows, full history, the year strip, all four urge tools, caffeine logging, every theme, the world and backup. Premium adds trigger insights, world themes with extra trees and decor, a home-screen widget on Android and CSV export. The yearly plan starts with a one-week free trial for new subscribers, and a monthly plan is also available. You see the price before setup.

Use Grovly as a nail biting tracker, a skin picking tracker or a hair pulling tracker, or for all three at once. It is a self-help tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition.
