Cuddle Pop Privacy Policy

Cuddle Pop ("the app") is a play app for young children (about 2 to 6), made by Can Albayrak ("we"). We designed it to collect as little as possible.

iOS and iPadOS · Android

iOS and iPadOS

Effective November 19, 2026.

We do not collect or share personal information or gameplay data through the iOS and iPadOS app. Gameplay works offline without an account. The app has no ads, analytics, crash reporting service, in-app purchases, or cross-app tracking. It does not access advertising identifiers or ask for your name, email address, or location.

The app saves sound, music and vibration settings, stars, and stickers on your device. The app keeps this data so you can continue where you left off and does not send it to us. To remove the local copy of your saved data, use Delete App on your device.

Grown-ups can open this privacy policy in their web browser from the grown-ups area after passing the parental gate. Opening the page requires an internet connection. Children can play without opening a website.

Grown-ups may email canalbayrak.games@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information they have shared with us directly. We use that correspondence to handle the request. If you believe a child shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can review and delete it.

Android

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Short version

We do not collect any personal information ourselves: there is no account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or location. The app shows a small number of child-safe ads between rounds through Google AdMob, with non-personalized advertising only and without using the advertising ID. Parents can remove the ads with a one-time purchase behind a parental gate. The app has no analytics service and no crash reporting.

What the app stores

Sound, music and vibration settings, the stars earned and the stickers collected are saved only on the device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

  • No accounts, no chat, no social features.
  • The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, contacts or location.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone or files.

Advertising

The app shows a limited number of full-screen ads at natural breaks (after a celebration, never during play, never in the first two minutes) through Google AdMob.

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children (child-directed treatment and "under the age of consent").
  • Ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • Ads are non-personalized. The app does not read, use or collect the advertising ID (the Android advertising ID or the iPhone IDFA), and no advertising ID is sent with the ad request.
  • To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address, device type and operating system, and app interactions with the ad. See Google's policy for child-directed ad requests: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431 and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • We never receive this ad data; Google keeps it under its own retention rules, and we do not share anything else with anyone.

Purchases

A one-time "Remove ads" purchase is available only in the grown-ups area, behind a parental gate, and is processed by Google Play. We do not receive payment details.

Links

The app contains no links to websites or social media in the areas children use.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads and to check the "Remove ads" purchase.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback. Grown-ups can turn it off.

Children

The app is made for young children (about 2 to 6) and follows the Google Play Families policy and the US COPPA rules: no account, no name, email, photo, voice recording or location, and no advertising ID. Every ad request is child-directed and ads are non-personalized, as described above. If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, write to canalbayrak.games@gmail.com and we will check and delete it.

Your rights

  • You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask for correction or deletion: write to canalbayrak.games@gmail.com and we reply.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device.
  • The only data outside our control is what Google processes to serve and measure ads; Google's privacy policy above explains the controls it offers.

Parents' rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to review or delete. There is no account to close and nothing is stored on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored settings and stars. Contact us at canalbayrak.games@gmail.com with any question about this policy or your child's privacy.

Laws we follow

We design the app to comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR (including the UK Age Appropriate Design Code), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy linked from the app's store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your child's privacy: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com
