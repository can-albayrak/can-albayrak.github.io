Cuddle Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Cuddle Pop ("the app") is a play app for young children (about 2 to 6), made by Can Albayrak ("we"). We designed it to collect as little as possible.

Short version

We do not collect any personal information. The app shows a small number of child-safe ads between rounds through Google AdMob, with personalized advertising turned off. Parents can remove the ads with a one-time purchase behind a parental gate.

What the app stores

Sound, music and vibration settings, the stars earned and the stickers collected are saved only on the device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

  • No accounts, no chat, no social features.
  • The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, contacts or location.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone or files.

Advertising

The app shows a limited number of full-screen ads at natural breaks (after a celebration, never during play, never in the first two minutes) through Google AdMob.

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children (child-directed treatment and "under the age of consent").
  • Ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • Personalized and interest-based advertising is disabled. The app does not access the advertising ID.
  • To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address, device type and operating system, and app interactions with the ad. See Google's policy for child-directed ad requests: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431 and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

A one-time "Remove ads" purchase is available only in the grown-ups area, behind a parental gate, and is processed by Google Play. We do not receive payment details.

Links

The app contains no links to websites or social media in the areas children use.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads and to check the "Remove ads" purchase.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback. Grown-ups can turn it off.

Parents' rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to review or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored settings. Contact us at canalbayrak.games@gmail.com with any question about this policy or your child's privacy.

Laws we follow

We design the app to comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR (including the UK Age Appropriate Design Code), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy linked from the app's store listing.
