Cuddle Pop: Toddler Games

Gentle tap-and-pop games for toddlers: big shapes, soft sounds, no reading needed. Made for parents of 1-4 year olds.

Coming soon to Google Play In development

How to play

A happy play box for little hands. No reading, no timers, no losing: just tap, pop and giggle.

Seven Toys In One

  • Bubble Pop: tap bubbles and animal friends jump out. Quick pops climb a musical scale.
  • Fireworks: tap the sky to launch rockets, or draw with a sparkler and watch your drawing burst into light.
  • Monster Munch: feed Poppy the monster the food it wants. Wrong food? A head shake and a giggle. Big BURP at the end!
  • Animal Band: a rainbow xylophone with an animal singer on every bar. Play freely or follow the glowing bar to play a little tune.
  • Paint Splat: finger painting with candy colors, a rainbow brush, stamps and glitter on 24 coloring pages.
  • Sticker Book: collect 54 stickers, one after every celebration.
  • And a playful home screen where balloons pop and letters sing.

Made For Little Kids

  • Big, bright, easy targets for small fingers.
  • A friendly hand shows what to do when a child pauses.
  • Every success fills the star meter for a big celebration.
  • Works offline.

For Grown-Ups

  • Sound, music and vibration settings and "Remove ads" are behind a parental gate.
  • The app shows a few child-safe ads between rounds, never during play. Personalized ads are turned off.
  • A one-time purchase removes all ads.
  • Grown-ups screens in English, Spanish, French, Hebrew, Turkish and German.

Questions

What is Cuddle Pop?

Gentle tap-and-pop games for toddlers: big shapes, soft sounds, no reading needed. Made for parents of 1-4 year olds.

Is Cuddle Pop free?

Yes. Cuddle Pop is free on Google Play. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one for a bonus; there are no banner ads.

Does Cuddle Pop work offline?

Yes, you can play without an internet connection.

Which languages does Cuddle Pop support?

English, Turkish.

Who is Cuddle Pop for?

Toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 6, and the parents who play with them. It follows the Google Play Families policy.

When is Cuddle Pop released?

It is in development. Follow WildCat Studios on YouTube for the release.

What is Cuddle Pop made with?

Godot 4, by a one-person studio, WildCat Studios.
