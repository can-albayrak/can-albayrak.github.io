Kadro Açık Artırması Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Kadro Açık Artırması ("the game") is a football squad auction game for Android made by Can Albayrak ("we").

Short version

The game has no ads, no analytics and no real-money purchases. To play online it creates a guest account on our own game server: a nickname, a random device key and your game results. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number or location, and we never sell or share your data.

Guest account (our game server)

When you open the game, it connects to our game server and creates a guest account. The server stores:

  • your nickname and its 4-digit tag (for example Can#1234), which you choose and can change in Settings;
  • a random key that identifies your device (only a hash of it is stored);
  • online game data: Elo ratings, season and weekly rankings, wins, draws and losses, your last 30 online matches (squads, prices, scores), achievements, coins, daily rewards and the cosmetic items you bought with coins;
  • your friends list and friend requests.

Other players can see your nickname, tag, Elo, rankings, online status (online, in a match, offline) and, in matches, your squad. Players can only send each other quick reactions from a fixed list; there is no free-text chat.

The server is a rented virtual server in Istanbul, Türkiye. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Your IP address is used to connect you and is not saved in your account; it may appear briefly in the server's technical logs.

The data is kept until you ask us to delete it (see below).

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, ambience, vibration, reduced motion), bot match statistics, local tournament progress, the tutorial state and the device key are saved only on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Squad images you save from the result screen stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all its data from our server, email canalbayrak.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and your nickname with its tag (shown in Settings). We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device.

What the game does not do

  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services.
  • No real-money purchases: coins are earned by playing and only buy cosmetic items.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to the game server for online matches, friends and rankings.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Player names and statistics

The game uses names and career statistics of real footballers and basketball players as public sports facts. It does not use their photos, and it is not endorsed by any player, club or league.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Laws

We design the game to comply with the EU/UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy linked from the store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com

Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

Yürürlük tarihi: 29 Ekim 2026

Kısaca

Oyunda reklam, analiz aracı ve gerçek parayla satın alma yoktur. Çevrim içi oynamak için kendi oyun sunucumuzda misafir hesap açılır: takma ad, rastgele bir cihaz anahtarı ve oyun sonuçların. Gerçek adını, e-postanı, telefonunu veya konumunu istemeyiz; verini satmayız ve kimseyle paylaşmayız.

Sunucuda tutulanlar

  • Takma adın ve etiketin (ör. Can#1234); Ayarlar'dan değiştirebilirsin.
  • Cihazını tanıyan rastgele anahtarın özeti.
  • Çevrim içi oyun verisi: Elo, sezon ve haftalık sıralama, galibiyet/beraberlik/mağlubiyet, son 30 çevrim içi maç, başarımlar, coin, günlük ödüller ve coin'le alınan görünüm öğeleri.
  • Arkadaş listen ve arkadaşlık istekleri.

Diğer oyuncular takma adını, etiketini, Elo'nu, sıralamanı, çevrim içi durumunu ve maçta kadronu görür. Oyuncular yalnız sabit listeden hazır tepki gönderebilir; serbest yazışma yoktur. Sunucu İstanbul'da kiralık bir sanal sunucudur; bağlantı şifrelidir. IP adresin yalnız bağlantı için kullanılır, hesabına kaydedilmez.

Cihazında kalanlar

Ayarların, bot maç istatistiklerin, yerel turnuva, tanıtım durumu ve cihaz anahtarı yalnız telefonunda durur; oyunu silince silinir.

Verini silme

Hesabını ve sunucudaki bütün verisini sildirmek için "Hesap silme" konusuyla ve Ayarlar'da görünen etiketinle canalbayrak.games@gmail.com adresine yaz. 30 gün içinde silip haber veririz.
