Sold! Soccer Squad Builder

A two-player football auction: bid on real footballers with the same budget, build a squad for the challenge and let real career stats decide the winner.

Coming soon to Google Play In development

How to play

Same budget, same challenge, one rival. Footballers go up for auction one by one: "Who gives 3?" Bid, pass, snatch them at the last second!

When both squads are full, real stats are revealed one by one. The squad that best fits the challenge wins.

⚽ HOW TO PLAY

  • Both sides get the same budget and a challenge: most goals, most assists, most matches, most cards...
  • Footballers start at $1 and you take turns bidding.
  • Spend everything on one star or collect cheap surprises? Your call.
  • At the end, career stats are revealed, points are added up and the winner is crowned.

🏆 MODES

  • Ranked: Elo rating, 4-week seasons, weekly and season leaderboards.
  • Play with friends: create a room with an invite code, ask for a rematch.
  • Two players on one phone: no internet needed.
  • Against a bot: the Gambler, the Saver and the Analyst.
  • Tournaments: 3-8 players, knockout or league + playoffs, with friends or bots.
  • Fun modes: Powers (Magnifier, Lock, Swap) and Draft (steal a player from your rival).
  • NBA mode: the same thrill with basketball legends.

📊 REAL STATS

Club and international careers of hundreds of current footballers. Choose a Türkiye, Europe or mixed player pool. Every match mixes stars, solid names and surprises.

👥 WITH FRIENDS

  • Friends list, invite to a match with one tap.
  • Watch your friend's match live.
  • Tease your rival with quick reactions.
  • Share both squads as an image after the match.
  • Fill your collection album and earn achievements.

🎁 COMPLETELY FREE

No ads, no real-money purchases. Earn coins by playing; the shop only has cosmetic items that never affect the match.

This game is not endorsed by any footballer, club or league; player names and statistics are public sports facts.
