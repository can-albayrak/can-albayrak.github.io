Tilt Grid: Rotate Block Puzzle

A gravity block puzzle: rotate the whole board so blocks fall, lines clear and chains grow. Short offline sessions.

In closed testing on Google Play Closed test

How to play

Place the blocks. Tilt the board. Let gravity do the rest.

Tilt Grid is a block puzzle with gravity. Drag pieces onto the 8x8 grid and fill a row or a column to clear it. Then comes the twist: spend a Tilt to rotate the whole board 90 degrees. Every loose piece falls to the new bottom, full lines clear, everything above falls again, and one smart tilt can set off a chain of clears across the grid.

How A Tilt Works

  1. Set up the board: leave gaps where you want the pieces to land.
  2. Hold a tilt button to preview the result before you commit.
  3. Rotate left or right. Gravity does the rest: pieces fall, lines clear, more fall.
  4. Every extra step in the chain multiplies your score: x2, x3, x4...

Why You Will Love It

  • Easy to learn: drag, drop, clear. Deep once gravity joins in.
  • Tilts are a resource: clear lines to earn more, then spend them at the right moment.
  • No timer and no rush. Think ahead, rotate, and watch the cascade.
  • A fresh take on the classic block game: every board can be turned four ways.

Two Ways To Play

  • Endless: how high can you score before the grid fills up?
  • Levels: 100 puzzle levels with goals, gray blocks to break and a limited number of pieces.

Also

  • Plays offline, no account needed.
  • Six color themes that change as you play.
  • Seven languages: English, German, Turkish, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Arabic.
  • Short games, perfect for a quick break.

If you like brain games, logic puzzles and rotate puzzles, give the board a tilt.
