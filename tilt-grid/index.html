Tilt Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Tilt Grid ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android made by Can Albayrak ("we").

Short version

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

What the game stores

The game saves on your own device only: unlocked levels, your best score, simple play statistics (games played, play time, longest chain) and your settings (sound, vibration, language, whether you saw the tutorial). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • No internet connection is used by the game.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds ads, purchases or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the update will describe what data those services use.
