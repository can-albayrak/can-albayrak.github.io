Triangle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Triangle (Turkish: ÜÇGEN, "the game") is a puzzle game for Android and iPhone made by Can Albayrak ("we"). This policy applies from version 0.13.0.

Short version

We do not collect your name, email or any account. The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels or menus, and optional rewarded ads for an extra hint. The game itself has no analytics, no Firebase, no remote config and no server of its own; your progress and settings stay on your device.

What the game stores

The game saves on your own device only: your level progress, your settings (language, sound, vibration, reduced motion, tutorial) and limited local diagnostics. Nothing is sent to a server of ours. Android backup is disabled. Resetting progress in Settings removes progress but keeps your preferences. The data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data; there is no server recovery.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads appear only between levels or menus, never during play. There are none in the first 16 levels and none before 10 minutes of active play; after that, at least 10 active minutes pass between two ads, with at most 2 ads per 30 minutes and 3 per app session.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: the first hint for each level per session is free. An extra hint on the same level can be unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded ad; the reward is given only when the ad is completed.
  • There are no banner ads and no in-app purchases. Only general-audience (G-rated) ads are requested.
  • To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID (on iPhone, the IDFA, only with your permission), your IP address (approximate location), device model and operating system, how you interact with ads, and diagnostics.
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad request. You can change your choice in Settings > Ad Privacy Options; the button appears only where required. Without consent, Google may show limited, non-personalized ads.
  • iPhone: Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown through Google's consent flow. The IDFA is used only if you tap Allow; you can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: AdMob may use the advertising ID. You can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Offline play

The puzzles play offline. Ads need an internet connection and simply do not show when you are offline.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, no Firebase, no remote config and no server of its own.
  • No banner ads.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state (Android): to load and show ads. The game itself plays offline.
  • Advertising ID (Android): the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission is merged by the Google Mobile Ads SDK; see the Android opt-out above. The SDK also adds WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE (basic network type only; the game cannot read your phone number, calls or contacts).
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • iPhone: the game asks only for tracking permission (above), and only for ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (target audience 13+), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Laws

We design the game to comply with the EU/UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy linked from the store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com

If you voluntarily email support, your email address and submitted content are used only to respond to your request, not intentionally sold or used for advertising. Support correspondence is retained for at most 90 days after the request closes unless legally required otherwise; contact the same address to request deletion. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.
