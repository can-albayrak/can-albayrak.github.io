Privacy Policy — ÜÇGEN / TRIANGLE

Updated: 16 November 2026 Contact: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com Versions before 0.13.0 contain no ads and collect no data. This policy covers ÜÇGEN / TRIANGLE from version 0.13.0. There are no accounts or in-app purchases, and the game does not ask you for personal information. Ads: the game shows ads from Google AdMob between levels and, if you choose, a rewarded ad for an extra hint. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device model and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change it any time under Settings > Ad Privacy Options. On iPhone, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking. Only general-audience ads are requested. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Progress, language, sound, vibration, motion and tutorial preferences and limited diagnostics stay on the device and are not sent to us. Resetting progress in Settings removes progress but keeps preferences. Uninstalling removes local records; there is no server recovery. If you voluntarily email support, your email address and message are used only to answer you and kept at most 90 days after the request closes unless the law requires otherwise. Do not send passwords. If data practices change, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before release.
