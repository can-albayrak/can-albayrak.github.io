Privacy Policy — ÜÇGEN / TRIANGLE

Updated: 17 November 2026 Contact: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com This policy covers ÜÇGEN / TRIANGLE from the version that adds ads (the release after 0.12.0-beta). The game itself has no account, no analytics, no Firebase, no remote config and no server of its own; it does not collect your name or email. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) on Android and iPhone, which processes some device data as described below. Progress, language, sound, vibration, motion and tutorial preferences and limited diagnostics stay on the device. They are not sent to a server of ours. Android backup is disabled. Resetting progress in Settings removes progress but retains preferences. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local records. No server recovery is available. Full-screen (interstitial) ads appear only between levels or menus, never during play. There are none in the first 16 levels and none before 10 minutes of active play; after that they are at least 10 active minutes apart, at most 2 per 30 minutes and 3 per app session. There are no banner ads. Rewarded ads are optional: the first hint for each level per session is free; an extra hint on the same level can be unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded ad. The reward is given only when the ad is completed. There are no in-app purchases. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID (on iPhone the IDFA, only with your permission), IP address (approximate location), device model and operating system, how you interact with ads, and diagnostics. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad request. You can change your choice in Settings > Privacy options; the button appears only where required. Without consent Google may show limited, non-personalized ads. iPhone: Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown through Google's consent flow. The IDFA is used only if you tap Allow; you can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Android: AdMob may use the advertising ID; you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android settings (Google > Ads). The puzzles play offline. Ads need an internet connection and simply do not show when you are offline. Android permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, plus the permissions the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds: the advertising ID (AD_ID), WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE (basic network type only; no phone number, calls or contacts). Vibration can be turned off in Settings. If you voluntarily email support, your email address and submitted content are used only to respond to your request, not intentionally sold or used for advertising. Support correspondence is retained for at most 90 days after the request closes unless legally required otherwise. Contact the same address to request deletion. Email providers process communications under their own terms. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. The game is not directed at children under 13 (target audience 13+) and does not ask children for personal information. The publisher selects the actual target audience in Play Console; this policy does not assign an age rating. If data practices change, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before release.
