Privacy Policy — ÜÇGEN / TRIANGLE

Updated: 29 October 2026 Contact: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com This policy covers the offline, ad-free 0.9.9-beta version of ÜÇGEN / TRIANGLE. The game does not automatically collect personal data or share it with third parties. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, in-app purchases or downloaded language packs. Translations are bundled. Progress, language, sound, vibration, motion and tutorial preferences and limited diagnostics stay on the device. They are not automatically transmitted to a server. Android backup is disabled. Resetting progress in Settings removes progress but retains preferences. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local records. No server recovery is available. If you voluntarily email support, your email address and submitted content are used only to respond to your request, not intentionally sold or used for advertising. Support correspondence is retained for at most 90 days after the request closes unless legally required otherwise. Contact the same address to request deletion. Email providers process communications under their own terms. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. The game does not ask children for personal information. The publisher selects the actual target audience in Play Console; this policy does not assign an age rating. If data practices change, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before release.
