SpiderBot: Neon Run Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

SpiderBot: Neon Run ("the game") is an endless runner game for Android made by Can Albayrak ("we").

Short version

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game can show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. That Google service processes some device data as described below.

What the game stores

The game saves on your own device only: your best score, your in-game credits (an in-game currency that cannot be bought or cashed out), unlocked costumes and upgrades, your power-up stock, whether you finished the tutorial, the times of the rewarded ads watched in the last hour, and your settings (sound, music, language and display options). We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Ads are optional: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one, to continue a run once, to double the credits of a finished run, or to get credits in the shop. At most 10 an hour. There are no banner ads and no ads between runs.
  • To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with the ad.
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. Where you do not consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalized ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics of our own.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself plays offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Laws

We design the game to comply with the EU/UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy linked from the store listing.
