How to play

Run the rooftops. Swing across the gaps. Stay ahead of the dark.

SpiderBot: Neon Run is a cyberpunk parkour runner high above a neon city. Your robot courier sprints across the rooftops on its own: swipe to jump and slide, hold WEB to swing from glowing rings, and chain clean moves to raise your multiplier. Something dark is chasing you, and three quick mistakes in a row let it catch up.

Moves That Flow

  • Swipe up to jump and vault, swipe down to slide under pipes and lasers.
  • Hold WEB to swing from ring to ring; release on the upswing to fly further.
  • Web the yellow hooks to zip up to high roofs, and climb glowing wall shafts.
  • Slam two drones into each other, catch rockets and energy cells and hurl them at laser gates.
  • Fill the EXO bar with clean running and transform into heavy armour that smashes through.

An Endless City

  • Every run builds a new route of rooftops, steam vents, falling signs and moving bars.
  • Dimensional rifts pull you into a void of floating crystals until the exit portal brings you back.
  • Dawn turns to day, dusk and night as you run deeper into the city.

Make It Yours

  • Earn credits on every run and spend them on ten suits, two city themes and upgrades for EXO, shield, magnet, turbo, web and credits.
  • An 8-step tutorial teaches every move, and you can replay it any time.

Also

  • Plays offline, no account needed (ads need a connection).
  • Optional rewarded ads: revive once, double a run's credits, or earn credits in the shop.
  • Seven languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic and German.

If you like parkour games, cyberpunk games and endless runners, take to the rooftops.
