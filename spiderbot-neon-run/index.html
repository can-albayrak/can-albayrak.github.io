How to play

A neon rooftop runner: a spider robot swings, climbs and parkours across a cyberpunk city.

In this endless runner your robot sprints on its own: swipe to jump and slide, tap WEB to swing from glowing rings, and chain clean moves to push your multiplier up to x5. Something dark is chasing you. One crash puts it right on your back; crash again too soon and it catches you.

Parkour That Flows

  • Swipe up to jump and vault, swipe down to slide under pipes, lasers and drones.
  • Hold WEB to swing from ring to ring. Let go on the upswing to fly further, and flip over the big drops.
  • Web the yellow hooks to zip up to the highest roof.
  • Climb glowing wall shafts: web one wall, then the other, and pull yourself over the top.
  • Charge a long jump on the lit strip and pounce across the widest gaps.

Robot Action

  • Web two linked attack drones and slam them into each other.
  • Catch rockets and energy cells, then drag and release to hurl them at laser gates.
  • Fill the EXO bar with clean running and transform into heavy armour that smashes through everything.
  • Fire up boost thrusters, shield panels and the credit magnet when you need them.

An Endless Cyberpunk City

  • Every run deals a fresh route from more than 200 rooftop sections, with short breathers between the hard parts.
  • Dimensional rifts pull you into a void of floating crystals until the exit portal brings you back.
  • The neon city shifts through six colour themes the deeper you run.

Make It Yours

  • Earn credits on every run, plus a daily bonus that grows with your streak.
  • Spend them on suits, nitro trails, EXO looks and upgrades for EXO, shield, magnet, turbo, web and credits. Every upgrade can be earned just by playing.
  • A 9-step tutorial teaches every move. Replay it from the main menu any time.

Good To Know

  • Plays offline, no account needed (ads need a connection).
  • No in-app purchases. Optional rewarded ads only: revive once per run, double a run's credits, or collect credits in the shop up to 5 times a day.
  • 12 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Dutch, Indonesian and Arabic.

If you like parkour games, robot games and endless runners, download SpiderBot and take to the rooftops.
