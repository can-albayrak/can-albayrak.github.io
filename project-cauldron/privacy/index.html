Slime Alchemy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Slime Alchemy (working title Project Cauldron, "the app") is a puzzle game made by Can Albayrak, WildCat Studios ("we").

Short version

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it plays fully offline.

What the app stores

Your progress (levels, stars, recipes) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, colour symbols, language) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

  • No accounts, no chat, no social features.
  • The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone or files.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state are declared in the Android package for future updates; the current version does not send or receive any data.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The app is made for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we add features that process data (for example advertising), we will update this policy, change the date above and update the store listing before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: canalbayrak.games@gmail.com
