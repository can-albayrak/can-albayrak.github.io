How to play

Send the blobs. Blast the cubes. Reveal the picture.

Slime Alchemy is a color shooter puzzle starring cute pixel-art goo friends. Every board is a pixel picture built from colored blocks. Tap a blob to send it onto the belt: it rides around the board and shoots the tiles of its own color. Clear the board and the picture underneath becomes a finished potion for your grimoire.

How To Play

  1. Tap a blob to send it around the belt.
  2. Each blob shoots the outermost tiles that match its color.
  3. A blob with shots left over waits in a jar on the shelf.
  4. Fill every jar and the brew is lost, so choose the order with care.

Why You Will Love It

  • A cozy pixel puzzle that still makes you think: every blob carries exact ammo.
  • 200 levels in 10 chapters, each one a new pixel picture to reveal.
  • Crystal blocks, seals and caged jars keep every chapter fresh.
  • Three alchemy powers: Sulfur cracks a crystal in one hit, Quicksilver splashes the tile next door, Elixir gives a blob two extra shots.
  • Master levels open up as you collect stars.
  • Short levels and no timer. Plays offline.

A Cube Shooter With A Potion Twist

If you enjoy color shooter and cube shooter puzzles, this slime puzzle game adds a cauldron of charm: goo buddies with faces, glowing potions, and a recipe book that fills up as you play.
