How to play

Draw a fuse. Light it. Watch the chain reaction spread.

A hex puzzle with a fuse: draw a path through gems, light it and set off a chain reaction. Short levels, plays offline.

Drag across 2 to 5 touching gems to draw a fuse, then let go: the spark runs along it, and every burning one sets its same-color neighbors on fire, wave after wave. One clever path can burn half the board.

How It Works

  1. Draw a short fuse through touching gems of any color.
  2. Let go and the spark lights everything on the path.
  3. Fire jumps to touching gems of the same color, and from them to the next.
  4. Burned pieces disappear, new ones drop in, and your score climbs with every wave.

Why It Feels Different

  • One simple move, a huge payoff: plan the fuse that links the biggest color groups.
  • Chain reactions that keep growing: x10, x20, INFERNO!
  • Powder kegs blow up every neighbor, whatever the color.
  • Stones block the fire: crack them, then break them before they take over.

Two Ways To Play

  • Levels: 200 hex puzzle levels with goals: reach a score, burn certain colors or break every stone before your moves run out. Earn up to three stars on each.
  • Endless: stones fall faster and faster. How long can you hold out?

Boosters

Undo a move, smash a stone with the hammer or get five more moves. Pay with coins you earn by playing, or watch an optional ad. Coin packs are sold in the shop.

Also

  • Plays offline, no account needed (ads and the shop need a connection).
  • Seven languages: English, German, Turkish, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Arabic.
  • Short levels, made for a quick break.
  • Ads: you can choose to watch an ad for a booster or coins, and an ad may show between games.
