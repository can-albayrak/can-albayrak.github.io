Fuse Chain Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Fuse Chain ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android made by Can Albayrak ("we").

Short version

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads, and a full-screen ad between games), sells coin packs through Google Play and sends game events to Google Firebase Analytics. Those Google services process some device data as described below.

What the game stores

The game saves on your own device only: unlocked levels, stars, best scores, coins, boosters, the times of the rewarded ads watched in the last hour, how many ad offers you saw and took, the length and score of your recent endless games, a random install ID made by the game, the purchases already paid out, and your settings (sound, vibration, language, whether you saw the tutorial). We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Game settings download

When you open the game, it downloads a small settings file from our website (can-albayrak.github.io, hosted by GitHub). It contains game tuning values only. Like any web request, this shares your IP address with GitHub; no other data is sent. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Difficulty

In endless mode, the game adjusts how fast new stones arrive based on how you play: a little slower for new players and after several quick losses, a little faster when you play long games. It never uses ad data, your location or your device for this. Levels are the same for everyone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Rewarded ads are optional: one plays only when you choose "watch an ad" for a booster or coins, at most 10 an hour.
  • Full-screen ads between games: when you leave a finished game or level (Next level, Replay, Play again or the menu), a full-screen ad may show, never during play. There are no banner ads.
  • To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with the ad.
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. Where you do not consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalized ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

  • The game sends game events to Firebase Analytics so we can improve it and see which game settings and ads work better: games started and ended (score, moves, length), ad offers and whether an ad was watched, which ad loaded, the ad revenue reported by AdMob, and the test group of game settings you are in.
  • Firebase also collects an app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version, approximate country (from the IP address) and the advertising ID, and logs some events by itself (such as first open and session start).
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: analytics follows the same consent form as ads. Until then, and in those regions, analytics runs with consent denied: no identifiers are stored and Google only receives limited, anonymous signals.
  • Data is kept for 2 months in Google Analytics (Google's default) and is not sold.
  • Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs are sold and paid for through Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which pack was bought so the coins can be added.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics other than Firebase Analytics.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and the game settings file, to talk to Google Play for purchases, and to send analytics. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Laws

We design the game to comply with the EU/UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy linked from the store listing.
