Fuse Chain: Hex Chain Reaction

A hex puzzle with a fuse: draw a path through gems, light it and set off a chain reaction. Short levels, plays offline.

In closed testing on Google Play Closed test

How to play

Draw a fuse. Light it. Watch the chain reaction spread.

Fuse Chain is a fiery hex puzzle game. Drag across 2 to 5 touching gems to draw a fuse, then let go: the spark runs along it, and every burning one sets its same-color neighbors on fire, wave after wave. One clever path can burn half the board!

How It Works

  1. Draw a short fuse through touching gems of any color.
  2. Let go and the spark lights everything on the path.
  3. Fire jumps to touching gems of the same color, and from them to the next.
  4. Burned pieces disappear, new ones drop in, and your score climbs with every wave.

Why You Will Love It

  • A simple move with a huge payoff: plan the fuse that links the biggest color groups.
  • Chain reactions that grow and grow: x10, x20, INFERNO!
  • Powder kegs that blow up every neighbor, whatever the color.
  • Stones that block the fire: crack them, then break them before they take over.

Two Ways To Play

  • Levels: 100 hex puzzle levels with goals: reach a score, burn certain colors, or break every stone, before your moves run out. Earn up to three stars on each.
  • Endless: stones keep falling faster and faster. How long can you last?

Boosters

Undo a move, smash a stone with the hammer, or get five more moves. Pay with coins you earn by playing, or watch an optional ad. Coin packs are available in the shop.

Also

  • Plays offline, no account needed (ads and the shop need a connection).
  • Seven languages: English, German, Turkish, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic.
  • Short games, perfect for a quick break.

If you like gem puzzles, color puzzles and chain reaction games, download Fuse Chain and light your first fuse.
